Active Recovery
Foam roll + stretch + walk
~45 min
Same recovery routine as Month 1
Foam rolling (15 min): glutes/piriformis, quads/hip flexors, hamstrings, upper back. Stretching (15 min): 90/90 hip, couch stretch, figure-4, chest doorway — 60 sec holds. Walk (15 min): easy, outdoors, casual pace.
Month 2 addition: Your muscles are working harder now, so spend an extra 15–30 sec on any spot that's especially tender during foam rolling. If you've noticed one glute is more sore than the other after single-leg hip thrusts, give the weaker side extra attention.

Prep & Recovery
Meal prep + weekly review + Monday prep
~2 hrs
🍳 Meal Prep
Same structure: 2 proteins, 2 carbs, veggies, snacks. Check last week's prep — what ran out first? Make more of that.
📊 Weekly Review — Month 2 Focus Questions
  • Did you hit every target weight this week? If yes, move up. If you missed reps, stay put one more week.
  • Which exercise improved the most? Celebrate that. Write it down.
  • Which exercise feels stuck? That's the one to add a 4th set to instead of more weight.
  • Protein check: Did you average 100g+ most days?
  • Sleep check: 7+ hours? This matters more in month 2 because the loads are higher.
  • Body changes: How do clothes fit? Notice anything in the mirror? Take a progress photo if you haven't.
🧘 Light Stretching + Sleep Prep
Same as month 1: cat-cow, world's greatest stretch, child's pose, spinal twist, quad stretch. Pack gym bag. Set alarm for 7+ hrs sleep.

Glute Focus A
Heavy compounds + activation · hip-hinge dominant
~50 min
Warm-Up (5 min)
W
Banded Glute Bridges + Clamshells
2 × 15 each · light band · activation primer
Activation
▼
Glute Bridges:
  1. Lie on back, knees bent, feet flat hip-width. Band just above knees.
  2. Press feet into floor, drive hips up, push knees out against band.
  3. Squeeze glutes 1 sec at top, slowly lower.
Clamshells:
  1. Side-lying, knees bent 90°, feet stacked, band above knees.
  2. Open top knee toward ceiling keeping feet touching.
  3. Hold 1 sec, close slowly. All reps one side, then switch.
💡 Light resistance only — focus on feeling your glutes fire before the heavy work.
📊 EMG research confirms glute activation exercises before heavy lifts improve mind-muscle connection and increase glute recruitment in subsequent exercises.
Working Sets
1
Barbell Hip Thrust
3 × 10–12 · pause 2 sec at top · squeeze hard
Glutes
▼
  1. Upper back against bench. Roll barbell over hips (use bar pad).
  2. Feet flat, shoulder-width, knees at ~90° at the top.
  3. Drive through heels, squeeze glutes to push hips up until torso is parallel.
  4. Hold 2 seconds — squeeze as hard as you can.
  5. Lower slowly (3 sec). Don't let butt touch floor between reps.
💡 Start with just the bar or bodyweight. Keep chin tucked — don't arch neck.
📊 2025 Frontiers in Physiology meta-analysis: "When emphasizing GMax hypertrophy, single exercises such as the barbell hip thrust should be prioritized." Ranked as the #1 glute-building exercise across 12 studies.
2
Romanian Deadlift (Dumbbell)
3 × 10–12 · slow lowering · feel the hamstring stretch
Glutes + Hams
▼
  1. Stand hip-width, dumbbells in front of thighs, palms facing you.
  2. Soft bend in knees — push hips straight back.
  3. Lower dumbbells along shins until deep hamstring stretch (mid-shin).
  4. Keep back flat, chest proud, dumbbells close to legs.
  5. Drive hips forward to stand, squeeze glutes at top.
💡 Don't round your back. Shoulder blades pinched. Start 10–15 lb dumbbells.
📊 Research shows hip-hinge exercises with controlled eccentric (lowering) phase maximize hamstring + glute co-activation and stimulate hypertrophy through stretched-position loading.
3
Bulgarian Split Squat
3 × 10 each leg · dumbbells · lean slightly forward
Glutes
▼
  1. Stand ~2 ft in front of bench. One foot behind on bench, laces down.
  2. Dumbbell in each hand at sides.
  3. Lean torso forward ~15–20° (shifts emphasis to glutes).
  4. Bend front knee, lower until front thigh roughly parallel.
  5. Drive through front heel to stand. All reps one leg, switch.
💡 Balance will be shaky — start bodyweight or 5 lb dumbbells. Knee tracks over toes.
📊 PMC systematic review: single-limb squats elicit 82% MVIC in the gluteus maximus + 82% in gluteus medius — one of the highest dual-activation exercises measured.
4
Cable Kickbacks
3 × 12 each · squeeze at top 1 sec
Glutes
▼
  1. Ankle strap on low cable, around one ankle.
  2. Face machine, hold frame. Slight bend in standing leg.
  3. Core tight, back flat — kick strapped leg straight back.
  4. Squeeze glute 1 sec at top. Don't arch lower back.
  5. Slowly return. All reps, switch legs.
💡 Very light weight — isolation move. If you feel it in lower back, too heavy or arching.
📊 ACE research: quadruped hip extensions (same movement pattern) generated significantly more gluteus medius activation than squats, making kickbacks excellent for glute shaping.
5
Abductor Machine (Leaning Forward)
3 × 15 · lean forward 30° for upper glute
Glute Med
▼
  1. Sit in machine, feet on foot rests.
  2. Lean torso forward ~30° — targets upper glute (gluteus medius).
  3. Push knees apart in controlled motion.
  4. Hold 1 sec open, slowly bring knees back.
💡 The forward lean changes everything. Lower the weight vs. sitting upright.
📊 PMC meta-analysis: hip abduction exercises with external rotation generate 79–113% MVIC in the glute max — the forward lean shifts emphasis to the gluteus medius for that "shelf" look.
6
Dead Bug
2 × 10 each side · exhale fully · low back pressed down
Core
▼
  1. Lie on back, arms up, knees 90° (shins parallel to floor).
  2. Press lower back firmly into floor — zero gap.
  3. Extend right arm overhead + left leg out, hovering above floor.
  4. Exhale fully as you extend. Return, repeat opposite side.
💡 If lower back lifts off floor, shorten range of motion.
📊 2024 study (J. Functional Morphology & Kinesiology): dead bugs activate rectus abdominis, both obliques, transverse abdominis, multifidus, erector spinae, AND pelvic floor simultaneously — one of the most complete core exercises.

Arms & Shoulders
All 3 tricep heads + bicep peak & width
~45 min
Warm-Up (5 min)
W
Band Pull-Aparts + Arm Circles
2 × 15 each · shoulder mobility primer
Activation
▼
Band Pull-Aparts:
  1. Light band at shoulder height, arms straight, hands shoulder-width.
  2. Pull apart by squeezing shoulder blades until band touches chest.
  3. Slowly return. Arms straight the whole time.
Arm Circles:
  1. Arms out to sides. Small forward circles × 15, reverse × 15.
💡 Never skip — gets blood to rotator cuffs and prevents pressing injuries.
Working Sets
1
Dumbbell Shoulder Press
3 × 10–12 · seated · 3-sec lowering
Shoulders
▼
  1. Seated, back support. Dumbbells at shoulder height, palms forward.
  2. Press up until arms almost fully extended (don't lock).
  3. Lower to shoulders over 3 seconds.
💡 Start 8–10 lbs. Core braced, back flat against bench.
📊 EMG data: overhead press activates anterior deltoids at 79%, lateral deltoids at 66%, and triceps at 61% — outperforming isolation raises for overall shoulder development.
2
Concentration Curl
3 × 12 each arm · elbow braced on inner thigh
Biceps
▼
  1. Sit on bench, lean forward slightly. Brace back of upper arm against inner thigh.
  2. Dumbbell in hand, arm extended. Curl up toward shoulder.
  3. Squeeze bicep hard at top 2 sec. Lower over 3 sec.
  4. All reps one arm, switch.
💡 The thigh brace eliminates cheating — your bicep does 100% of the work. Start 8–10 lbs.
📊 ACE EMG study: concentration curls produced the highest biceps brachii activation of ALL exercises tested — significantly higher than barbell curls, EZ-bar curls, and preacher curls.
3
Overhead Tricep Extension (Cable Rope)
3 × 12 · deep stretch at bottom
Triceps (Long Head)
▼
  1. Rope on low pulley. Face away, grab rope, step forward.
  2. Lean slightly. Hands behind head, elbows pointing forward.
  3. Upper arms stay still — extend forearms overhead until arms nearly straight.
  4. Let weight slowly pull hands back — feel deep tricep stretch at bottom.
💡 The stretch at the bottom drives growth. Keep elbows forward, not flared.
📊 German EMG study: overhead extensions best activate the triceps LONG HEAD — the largest of the three heads and the one that gives arms that full, rounded look from behind.
4
Lateral Raises
3 × 15 · light weight · lead with elbows
Shoulders
▼
  1. Stand with light dumbbells at sides, slight elbow bend.
  2. Raise arms to sides to shoulder height — like pouring water.
  3. Lead with elbows (higher than wrists at top).
  4. Lower slowly (3 sec).
💡 5–8 lbs is plenty. If shoulders shrug to ears, too heavy.
5
Hammer Curls
3 × 12 · thumbs up · builds arm width
Biceps + Brachialis
▼
  1. Dumbbells at sides, palms facing body (thumbs up).
  2. Upper arms still — curl both up simultaneously.
  3. Squeeze at top, lower 3 seconds.
💡 Targets the brachialis — the muscle between bicep and tricep that adds WIDTH to your arms from the front view.
📊 The neutral grip shifts work from the biceps brachii to the brachialis and brachioradialis, providing a different growth stimulus than supinated (palms-up) curls. Pairing both in one session covers the full upper arm.
6
Triangle Push-Ups
3 × 8–12 · hands in diamond shape
Triceps (All 3 Heads)
▼
  1. Push-up position. Bring hands together under chest, thumbs and index fingers forming a triangle/diamond.
  2. Lower chest toward your hands, elbows tracking close to your body.
  3. Push back up. Keep core tight and body straight throughout.
  4. Too hard? Do them from your knees — same hand position.
💡 Start on knees if needed. Focus on slow descent (3 sec) and controlled push.
📊 ACE EMG study (Univ. of Wisconsin): triangle push-ups produced the HIGHEST triceps activation of all exercises tested — higher than dips, kickbacks, pushdowns, overhead extensions, and close-grip bench press.
7
Plank Hold
2 × 30 sec · squeeze everything · breathe
Core
▼
  1. Forearms on floor, elbows under shoulders.
  2. Straight line head to heels — no sagging or piking.
  3. Squeeze glutes, brace core, breathe normally. 30 sec.
💡 If 30 sec is too hard, do 15–20. Too easy? You're probably sagging.

Core & Conditioning
Deep core + rectus abdominis + obliques + cardio
~40 min
Core Circuit (3 rounds)
1
Pallof Press
12 each side · cable · anti-rotation
Deep Core
▼
  1. Cable at chest height, D-handle. Stand sideways, handle at chest with both hands.
  2. Step away until cable pulls you sideways. Feet shoulder-width, knees soft.
  3. Press handle straight out, arms extended. Resist the rotation — that IS the exercise.
  4. Hold 2 sec extended, bring back to chest. That's 1 rep.
💡 Best exercise for a flat stomach. Trains your transverse abdominis — your natural corset.
📊 2025 NSCA review: Pallof press ranks as a top anti-rotational core exercise, training the transverse abdominis and internal obliques that create the "pulled-in" midsection look.
2
Bicycle Crunches
15 each side · slow & controlled · no neck pulling
Rectus + Obliques
▼
  1. Lie on back, hands lightly behind ears (don't pull your neck).
  2. Lift shoulders off floor. Bring right elbow toward left knee while extending right leg.
  3. Rotate through your TORSO, not just your arms. Feel your obliques twist.
  4. Alternate sides in a slow, controlled pedaling motion. 1 second per side minimum.
💡 The biggest mistake is going fast. Slow down — 2 seconds per rep. Quality over speed.
📊 ACE EMG study: bicycle crunches produced the HIGHEST activation of both rectus abdominis and obliques of ALL 13 exercises tested — 248% more oblique activation than standard crunches.
3
Cable Woodchop
10 each side · rotate from torso
Obliques
▼
  1. Cable highest position, D-handle. Stand sideways, feet wide.
  2. Grab handle with both hands, arms toward cable.
  3. Rotate torso diagonally downward (high to low). Arms stay straight.
  4. Rotation from core, not arms. Hips mostly stable.
  5. Slowly return. All reps one side, switch.
💡 Slow and controlled. Don't yank.
4
Hanging Knee Raises
10 reps · no swinging · curl pelvis up
Lower Abs
▼
  1. Hang from pull-up bar, overhand grip, arms extended.
  2. Without swinging, slowly curl knees toward chest. Think: curl pelvis UP.
  3. Hold 1 sec at top, slowly lower to dead hang.
💡 No swinging = the whole point. Use captain's chair if grip is an issue.
📊 ACE research: captain's chair/hanging knee raises ranked #2 for rectus abdominis activation, just behind bicycle crunches — and #1 for the lower abs specifically.
Conditioning (15 min)
5
Incline Treadmill Walk
15 min · 10–12% incline · 3.0–3.5 mph · NO holding rails
Cardio + Glutes
▼
  1. 10–12% incline, 3.0–3.5 mph.
  2. Do NOT hold rails — removes glute and core benefit.
  3. Push through heels, torso upright.
  4. 15 minutes straight.
💡 Glute workout disguised as cardio. If you need to hold on, reduce speed slightly.

Glute Focus B
Squat-dominant + unilateral · higher volume
~50 min
Warm-Up (5 min)
W
Monster Walks + Fire Hydrants
2 × 12 each · band above knees
Activation
▼
Monster Walks:
  1. Band above knees. Quarter-squat position.
  2. Wide diagonal steps forward × 12, backward × 12.
Fire Hydrants:
  1. All fours, band above knees. Lift knee out to side, 90° bend.
  2. Lift until thigh parallel, squeeze, lower. All reps, switch.
💡 Keep hips level on fire hydrants — don't shift body sideways.
Working Sets
1
Step-Ups (Dumbbell)
3 × 10 each · knee-height box · drive through heel
Glutes
▼
  1. Bench/box at ~knee height. Dumbbell in each hand.
  2. Place ENTIRE right foot flat on box.
  3. Drive through right heel to step up. Only use top leg — don't push off floor.
  4. Stand fully on top, squeeze glute, slowly lower with control.
  5. All reps one leg, switch.
💡 Higher box = more glute. Don't push off ground with bottom foot. Lean slightly forward.
📊 PMC Systematic Review (2020): step-ups and variations showed the HIGHEST glute max activation of ALL exercises — averaging 125% MVIC, ranging 104–169% MVIC. This is your most important glute exercise on this day.
2
Sumo Squat (Goblet)
3 × 12 · wide stance · knees out
Glutes + Inner
▼
  1. Feet wide, toes at ~45°. Dumbbell vertical at chest (goblet grip).
  2. Push knees out, squat straight down. Torso upright.
  3. Go deep as comfortable, heels down, back straight.
  4. Drive through heels, squeeze glutes to stand.
💡 Wide stance shifts work to glutes + inner thighs. Start 15–20 lbs.
📊 2025 Frontiers meta-analysis: back squats at parallel or full ROM significantly enhance glute hypertrophy. The deep goblet squat achieves this ROM safely with beginner-appropriate loading.
3
Single-Leg Hip Thrust
3 × 10 each · bodyweight to start
Glutes
▼
  1. Upper back on bench, feet flat. Lift one foot (hold knee or extend leg).
  2. Drive through planted heel, hips up with one leg only.
  3. Squeeze glute 1 sec at top, lower slowly.
  4. All reps one side, switch.
💡 Start bodyweight. If one side is weaker (common!), start with that side.
📊 EMG research ranks single-leg hip thrust among the highest glute activators with minimal momentum, forcing the glute to produce all the power unilaterally — ideal for correcting asymmetries.
4
Cable Pull-Through
3 × 12 · hinge at hips · squeeze to stand
Glutes + Hams
▼
  1. Rope on low pulley. Straddle cable facing away.
  2. Grab rope between legs, walk forward for tension.
  3. Soft knees, hinge hips — push butt back.
  4. Feel hamstring stretch, squeeze glutes to drive hips forward.
  5. Arms don't work — everything comes from hips.
💡 Like an RDL with constant cable tension. Great for learning hip hinge. Keep back flat.
5
Frog Pumps
2 × 20 · soles together · burnout finisher
Glutes
▼
  1. Lie on back. Soles together, knees out (butterfly).
  2. Feet ~12–18 in. from glutes.
  3. Press through outer feet edges, drive hips up.
  4. Squeeze at top, lower, immediately repeat. Fast and pumpy.
💡 Finisher — burn by rep 10. No weight needed. Butterfly position forces glutes to do all the work.
6
Bird Dog
2 × 10 each · slow & controlled
Core
▼
  1. All fours — hands under shoulders, knees under hips.
  2. Extend right arm forward + left leg back simultaneously.
  3. Hold 2 sec. Arm, torso, leg form one line.
  4. Return, repeat opposite side.
💡 Zero torso movement. Water bottle test: place one on lower back — if it falls, too much rotation.
📊 2024 study: during hollowing maneuvers, bird dog produced the greatest increase in transverse abdominis thickness of all core stability exercises tested.

Full Body Sculpt
Compounds + arm & core finisher
~50 min
Working Sets
1
Goblet Squat
3 × 12 · sit deep · chest tall
Legs + Glutes
▼
  1. Dumbbell vertical at chest. Feet shoulder-width, toes out.
  2. Sit hips back and down. Chest tall, elbows inside knees.
  3. Thighs parallel or below.
  4. Drive through whole foot, squeeze glutes at top.
💡 The dumbbell counterbalances you — makes deep squats easier. 15–20 lbs to start.
📊 2025 meta-analysis: squats at parallel or full ROM significantly enhance glute hypertrophy. Goblet squat is the safest way to achieve full depth for beginners.
2
Dumbbell Row
3 × 10 each · pull to hip · squeeze back
Back
▼
  1. Left knee + hand on bench. Right foot on floor. Back flat.
  2. Dumbbell in right hand hanging down.
  3. Pull toward right HIP. Think "elbow to ceiling."
  4. Squeeze shoulder blade 1 sec. Lower slowly. Switch sides.
💡 Hip, not armpit — recruits lower lats for V-taper. 12–15 lbs to start.
3
Dumbbell Bench Press
3 × 10–12 · full range of motion
Chest + Triceps
▼
  1. Flat bench, dumbbell each hand at chest, palms facing feet.
  2. Shoulder blades together and down.
  3. Press up, arms nearly straight. Dumbbells slightly together at top.
  4. Lower slowly (3 sec), upper arms slightly below parallel.
💡 10–12 lbs to start. Shoulder blade squeeze = shoulder protection.
📊 EMG data: bench press achieves up to 95% MVC in pectoralis major, 79% in anterior deltoids, and 67% in triceps — the most efficient upper body compound movement.
4
Walking Lunges
3 × 10 each · long stride · lean forward
Glutes
▼
  1. Dumbbells at sides. BIG step forward.
  2. Lower back knee toward floor, both knees ~90°.
  3. Drive through front heel into next lunge.
  4. Alternate legs. 10 per leg = 20 steps.
💡 Longer stride + forward lean = more glute. 8–10 lbs to start.
📊 ACE EMG research: lunges generated significantly more gluteus medius activation than squats. The long stride + forward lean variant maximizes glute emphasis over quad.
Arm + Core Finisher
5
Bicep 21s
2 rounds · 7 bottom, 7 top, 7 full
Biceps
▼
  1. Dumbbells at sides.
  2. First 7: Bottom to halfway (elbows 90°), back down.
  3. Next 7: Halfway to shoulders, back to halfway.
  4. Final 7: Full curls bottom to top.
  5. Rest 60 sec, do round 2.
💡 Use ~60% of normal curl weight. Burns by the full-range set.
6
Tricep Rope Pushdowns
2 × 15 · spread rope at bottom
Triceps (Lateral)
▼
  1. Rope on high cable. Grab each end.
  2. Elbows pinned to sides, forearms parallel.
  3. Push down, straighten arms. At bottom SPREAD the rope and squeeze 1 sec.
  4. Slowly return. Only forearms move.
💡 The spread hits the lateral head — the visible part from the front.
📊 German EMG study: pushdowns best activate the lateral and medial triceps heads. Pairing with overhead extensions (Tue) covers all three heads across the week.
7
Ab Wheel Rollout
2 × 10 · slow extension · brace entire time
Core
▼
  1. Kneel on mat, grip wheel under shoulders.
  2. Brace core. Roll forward slowly.
  3. Only go as far as back stays flat (maybe only 1–2 ft at first).
  4. Pull back with abs.
Stability Ball version (easier):
  1. Kneel behind ball, forearms on top. Roll forward, roll back with abs.
💡 Start with stability ball. If lower back arches — you've gone too far.
📊 Journal of Orthopaedic & Sports Physical Therapy: the rollout was the MOST EFFECTIVE exercise for activating upper + lower rectus abdominis, external + internal obliques, and lats — while minimizing hip flexor and lumbar paraspinal involvement.

Key Principles
What the research says works
  • 🍑
    Glutes 2× per week, different patterns — Mon is hip-hinge dominant (hip thrusts, RDLs), Thu is squat-dominant (step-ups, sumo squats). 2025 meta-analysis: combined multi- and single-joint hip extension exercises significantly promote glute hypertrophy.
  • 🧠
    Mind-muscle connection is real — EMG research confirms that focusing on the target muscle during a lift increases its activation. A 2-sec squeeze at the top of hip thrusts measurably increases glute recruitment.
  • 🎯
    Flat stomach = deep core + nutrition — Pallof press and dead bugs train the transverse abdominis (your natural corset). Bicycle crunches and ab wheel rollouts hit the rectus abdominis and obliques. Calorie targets handle fat.
  • 💪
    Arms: cover all angles — Concentration curls (#1 EMG for biceps) + hammer curls (brachialis width). Triangle push-ups (#1 EMG for triceps) + overhead extensions (long head) + pushdowns (lateral head). Full coverage.
  • 📈
    Progressive overload — Every 1–2 weeks, add 2.5–5 lbs OR 1–2 reps. Track weights in your phone.
  • 😴
    Recovery — 7–8 hrs sleep. Sat & Sun off are part of the plan. Muscle grows during rest.

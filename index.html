Glute Focus A
Heavy glute compound movements + activation
~50 min
Warm-Up (5 min)
W
Banded Glute Bridges + Clamshells
2 × 15 each · light band · wake up those glutes
Activation
▼
Glute Bridges:
  1. Lie on your back, knees bent, feet flat hip-width apart. Place a resistance band just above your knees.
  2. Press feet into the floor and drive hips up, pushing knees out against the band.
  3. Squeeze glutes hard at the top for 1 sec, then slowly lower.
Clamshells:
  1. Lie on your side, knees bent at 90°, band above knees, feet stacked.
  2. Keeping feet touching, open your top knee toward the ceiling.
  3. Hold 1 sec at top, slowly close. All reps one side, then switch.
💡 Go light — focus on feeling your glutes fire, not on heavy resistance.
Working Sets
1
Barbell Hip Thrust
3 × 10–12 · pause 2 sec at top · squeeze hard
Glutes
▼
  1. Sit on the floor with upper back against a bench. Roll a barbell over your hips (use a bar pad).
  2. Feet flat, shoulder-width, knees at ~90° at the top.
  3. Drive through heels, squeeze glutes to push hips up until torso is parallel to the floor.
  4. Hold 2 seconds at top — squeeze as hard as you can.
  5. Lower slowly (3 sec down). Don't let your butt touch the ground between reps.
💡 Start with just the bar or bodyweight. Keep chin slightly tucked — don't arch your neck back.
2
Romanian Deadlift (Dumbbell)
3 × 10–12 · slow lowering · feel the stretch
Glutes + Hams
▼
  1. Stand hip-width, dumbbells in front of thighs, palms facing you.
  2. Soft bend in knees — push hips straight back like closing a car door with your butt.
  3. Lower dumbbells along shins until you feel a deep hamstring stretch (around mid-shin).
  4. Keep back flat, chest proud, dumbbells close to legs.
  5. Drive hips forward to stand, squeeze glutes at top.
💡 Don't round your back. Keep shoulder blades pinched. Start with 10–15 lb dumbbells.
3
Bulgarian Split Squat
3 × 10 each leg · dumbbells · lean slightly forward
Glutes
▼
  1. Stand ~2 feet in front of a bench. Place one foot behind you on the bench, laces down.
  2. Hold a dumbbell in each hand at your sides.
  3. Lean torso forward ~15–20° (shifts work to glutes instead of quads).
  4. Bend front knee, lower straight down until front thigh is roughly parallel to the floor.
  5. Drive through front heel to stand. All reps on one leg, then switch.
💡 Balance will be shaky at first — totally normal. Start bodyweight or 5 lb dumbbells. Front knee tracks over toes, don't let it cave inward.
4
Cable Kickbacks
3 × 12 each · squeeze at top for 1 sec
Glutes
▼
  1. Attach ankle strap to low cable pulley, strap around one ankle.
  2. Face machine, hold frame for balance. Slight bend in standing leg.
  3. Core tight, back flat — kick strapped leg straight back.
  4. Squeeze glute hard at top for 1 full second. Don't arch lower back.
  5. Slowly return to start. All reps, then switch legs.
💡 Very light weight — this is isolation. If you feel it in your lower back, you're going too heavy or arching.
5
Abductor Machine
3 × 15 · lean forward for upper glute emphasis
Glute Med
▼
  1. Sit in the machine, back against pad, feet on foot rests.
  2. Lean your torso forward ~30° — this targets upper glute (gluteus medius) instead of outer thigh.
  3. Push knees apart against pads in a controlled motion.
  4. Hold open position 1 sec, slowly bring knees back together.
💡 The forward lean makes a massive difference. You'll need to lower weight compared to sitting upright — that's expected.
6
Dead Bug
2 × 10 each side · exhale fully · press low back down
Core
▼
  1. Lie on back, arms straight up, knees bent 90° (shins parallel to floor).
  2. Press lower back firmly into the floor — zero gap.
  3. Slowly extend right arm overhead and left leg straight out, hovering above floor.
  4. Exhale fully as you extend — forces deep core to engage.
  5. Return to start, repeat opposite side.
💡 If your lower back lifts off the floor, you've gone too far. Shorten range of motion until stronger.

Arms & Shoulders
Sculpt biceps, triceps & shoulder caps
~45 min
Warm-Up (5 min)
W
Band Pull-Aparts + Arm Circles
2 × 15 each · get shoulders warm & mobile
Activation
▼
Band Pull-Aparts:
  1. Hold light band at shoulder height, arms straight, hands shoulder-width.
  2. Pull band apart by squeezing shoulder blades together until it touches your chest.
  3. Slowly return. Arms stay straight the whole time.
Arm Circles:
  1. Arms straight out to sides. Small forward circles × 15, then reverse × 15.
💡 This gets blood flowing to shoulders and rotator cuffs. Never skip — prevents injury on pressing moves.
Working Sets
1
Dumbbell Shoulder Press
3 × 10–12 · seated · control the negative
Shoulders
▼
  1. Sit on bench with back support. Dumbbells at shoulder height, palms forward.
  2. Press straight up until arms almost fully extended (don't lock elbows).
  3. Slowly lower to shoulder height over 3 seconds.
💡 Start 8–10 lbs. Don't arch your back — keep core braced, back flat against bench.
2
Dumbbell Bicep Curl
3 × 12 · alternate arms · no swinging
Biceps
▼
  1. Stand with dumbbells at sides, palms forward.
  2. Upper arm glued to your side — curl one dumbbell toward shoulder.
  3. Squeeze bicep at top 1 sec, lower slowly (3 sec). Alternate arms.
💡 If you need to swing your body, the weight is too heavy. Start 8–10 lbs.
3
Overhead Tricep Extension (Cable)
3 × 12 · rope attachment · stretch at bottom
Triceps
▼
  1. Rope on low cable pulley. Face away, grab rope with both hands.
  2. Step forward, lean slightly. Hands behind head, elbows pointing forward/up.
  3. Upper arms stay still — extend forearms up until arms nearly straight overhead.
  4. Let weight slowly pull hands back behind head — feel the tricep stretch at bottom.
💡 The stretch at the bottom is where growth happens. Don't rush. Keep elbows pointed forward, not flared.
4
Lateral Raises
3 × 15 · light weight · lead with elbows
Shoulders
▼
  1. Stand with light dumbbells at sides, slight elbow bend.
  2. Raise arms out to sides to shoulder height — like pouring two pitchers of water.
  3. Lead with elbows (elbows slightly higher than wrists at top).
  4. Lower slowly (3 sec). Don't drop them.
💡 These should be humblingly light — 5–8 lbs. If you're shrugging shoulders to your ears, too heavy.
5
Hammer Curls
3 × 12 · thumbs up grip · hits the brachialis
Biceps
▼
  1. Dumbbells at sides, palms facing your body (thumbs up — like holding hammers).
  2. Upper arms still — curl both up simultaneously.
  3. Squeeze at top, lower over 3 seconds.
💡 Hits the brachialis (between bicep and tricep) which gives arms a wider, more defined look from the front.
6
Tricep Dips (Assisted or Bench)
3 × 10 · slow descent · elbows back not flared
Triceps
▼
Bench version (recommended for beginners):
  1. Sit on bench edge, hands gripping edge beside hips, fingers forward.
  2. Slide butt off bench. Easier: bend knees. Harder: straight legs.
  3. Bend elbows straight BACK (not out!) — lower until upper arms parallel to floor.
  4. Press through palms to push back up.
💡 Keep back close to bench. If shoulders feel strained, reduce depth. Elbows go BACK, never to the sides.
7
Plank Hold
2 × 30 sec · squeeze everything · breathe
Core
▼
  1. Push-up position on forearms. Elbows under shoulders.
  2. Straight line from head to heels — no sagging or piking.
  3. Squeeze glutes, brace core, breathe normally. Hold 30 sec.
💡 If 30 sec is too hard, do 15–20. If too easy, you're probably sagging — check in a mirror.

Core & Conditioning
Flatten the midsection + build endurance
~40 min
Core Circuit (3 rounds)
1
Pallof Press
12 each side · cable · anti-rotation is key
Deep Core
▼
  1. Cable at chest height with D-handle. Stand sideways, hold handle at chest with both hands.
  2. Step away until cable tries to rotate you. Feet shoulder-width, knees soft.
  3. Press handle straight out until arms fully extended. The cable pulls you sideways — resist it. That IS the exercise.
  4. Hold 2 sec extended, slowly bring back to chest. That's 1 rep.
💡 Best core exercise for a flat stomach. Trains your transverse abdominis — your natural corset. Start very light.
2
Hanging Knee Raises
10 reps · no swinging · curl pelvis up
Lower Abs
▼
  1. Hang from pull-up bar, overhand grip, arms extended. (Ab sling straps work too.)
  2. Without swinging, slowly curl knees toward chest. Think: curl pelvis UP, not just lift knees.
  3. Hold top 1 sec, slowly lower back to dead hang.
💡 If you swing, it's worthless. Start from dead hang each rep. Can't hang? Use the captain's chair (arm rest station).
3
Cable Woodchop
10 each side · rotate through the torso
Obliques
▼
  1. Cable at highest position, D-handle. Stand sideways, feet wider than shoulders.
  2. Grab handle with both hands, arms extended toward cable.
  3. Rotate torso diagonally downward (high to low on opposite side). Arms stay relatively straight.
  4. Rotation from core, not arms. Hips stay mostly stable.
  5. Slowly return. All reps one side, then switch.
💡 Control the weight, don't yank it. Slow and controlled wins.
4
Plank Shoulder Taps
10 each side · keep hips still · no rocking
Stability
▼
  1. Push-up position, hands under shoulders, body straight.
  2. Widen feet for a stable base.
  3. Lift one hand, tap opposite shoulder, return. Repeat other side.
  4. Goal: hips stay STILL. No rocking side to side.
💡 Film yourself from behind — most people rock without realizing. Wider feet = easier. Go slow.
Conditioning (15 min)
5
Incline Treadmill Walk
15 min · 10–12% incline · 3.0–3.5 mph · no holding rails
Cardio + Glutes
▼
  1. Set treadmill to 10–12% incline, 3.0–3.5 mph.
  2. Walk naturally — do NOT hold rails! Holding removes glute and core benefit.
  3. Push through heels, torso upright (don't lean into incline).
  4. 15 minutes straight.
💡 Basically a glute workout disguised as cardio. The steep incline forces glutes to work every step. Need to hold on? Reduce speed slightly.

Glute Focus B
Higher volume · different angles · build the shape
~50 min
Warm-Up (5 min)
W
Monster Walks + Fire Hydrants
2 × 12 each · band above knees
Activation
▼
Monster Walks:
  1. Band above knees. Quarter-squat position (slight bend, chest up).
  2. Wide diagonal steps forward — like Frankenstein. Keep band tension constant.
  3. 12 steps forward, 12 steps backward.
Fire Hydrants:
  1. All fours (hands under shoulders, knees under hips), band above knees.
  2. Knee stays bent 90° — lift one knee out to side (like a dog at a hydrant).
  3. Lift until thigh parallel, squeeze glute, lower. All reps, then switch.
💡 Hips stay level during fire hydrants — don't shift your body to get the leg higher.
Working Sets
1
Sumo Squat (Goblet)
3 × 12 · wide stance · push knees out
Glutes + Inner
▼
  1. Feet significantly wider than shoulder-width, toes at ~45°.
  2. Hold one dumbbell vertically at chest (goblet grip).
  3. Push knees out over toes, squat straight down. Torso stays upright.
  4. Go deep as comfortable while heels stay down and back stays straight.
  5. Drive through heels, squeeze glutes to stand.
💡 Wide stance shifts work to glutes and inner thighs. Start 15–20 lb dumbbell.
2
Single-Leg Hip Thrust
3 × 10 each · bench supported · bodyweight to start
Glutes
▼
  1. Same setup as regular hip thrust — upper back on bench, feet flat.
  2. Lift one foot off ground (hold knee to chest or extend leg straight).
  3. Drive through planted heel, push hips up with one leg only.
  4. Squeeze glute hard at top 1 sec, lower slowly.
  5. All reps one side, then switch.
💡 Start bodyweight only — single-leg is much harder than expected. If one side is weaker, start with that side.
3
Cable Pull-Through
3 × 12 · hinge at hips · squeeze glutes to stand
Glutes + Hams
▼
  1. Rope on lowest pulley. Straddle cable facing away from machine.
  2. Grab rope between legs, walk forward a couple steps for tension.
  3. Soft knee bend, hinge at hips — push butt back. Cable pulls hands through legs.
  4. Feel hamstring stretch, then squeeze glutes hard to drive hips forward and stand tall.
  5. Arms don't do work — they just hold the rope. Everything comes from hips.
💡 Basically a Romanian deadlift with constant cable tension. Great for learning the hip hinge. Keep back flat.
4
Step-Ups (Dumbbell)
3 × 10 each · high box · drive through heel
Glutes
▼
  1. Stand in front of a bench/box (~knee height). Dumbbell in each hand.
  2. Place entire right foot flat on box — whole foot, not just toes.
  3. Drive through right heel to step up. Only use top leg — don't push off ground with bottom foot.
  4. Stand fully on top, squeeze right glute, slowly lower with control.
  5. All reps one leg, then switch.
💡 Higher box = more glute activation. Biggest mistake: pushing off the ground with bottom foot. Lean slightly forward for glute emphasis.
5
Frog Pumps
2 × 20 · soles together · burnout finisher
Glutes
▼
  1. Lie on back. Soles of feet together, knees out to sides (butterfly position).
  2. Pull feet ~12–18 inches from glutes.
  3. Press through outer edges of feet, drive hips up.
  4. Squeeze at top, lower, go right into next rep. Fast and pumpy.
💡 Finisher — glutes should burn by rep 10. No weight needed. The butterfly position forces glutes to do all the work.
6
Bird Dog
2 × 10 each · slow & controlled · brace core
Core
▼
  1. All fours — hands under shoulders, knees under hips.
  2. Brace core. Extend right arm forward + left leg back simultaneously.
  3. Hold 2 sec. Arm, torso, and leg form one straight line.
  4. Return to start. Repeat left arm + right leg.
💡 Zero torso movement is the goal. Place a water bottle on your lower back — if it falls, you're rotating too much.

Full Body Sculpt
Compound lifts + arm & core finisher
~50 min
Working Sets
1
Goblet Squat
3 × 12 · sit deep · chest tall
Legs + Glutes
▼
  1. Dumbbell vertical at chest, goblet grip. Feet shoulder-width, toes slightly out.
  2. Sit hips back and down like sitting into a chair. Chest tall, elbows inside knees.
  3. Go deep as comfortable (thighs parallel or below).
  4. Drive through whole foot to stand. Squeeze glutes at top.
💡 The dumbbell acts as a counterbalance — actually makes it easier to squat deep with good form. Start 15–20 lbs.
2
Dumbbell Row
3 × 10 each · pull to hip · squeeze back
Back
▼
  1. Left knee + left hand on bench. Right foot on floor. Back flat like a tabletop.
  2. Dumbbell in right hand hanging straight down.
  3. Pull toward right HIP (not armpit). Think "elbow to ceiling."
  4. Squeeze shoulder blade toward spine at top 1 sec.
  5. Lower slowly. All reps, switch sides.
💡 Pulling to hip (not chest) recruits lower lats for that V-taper shape. Start 12–15 lbs.
3
Dumbbell Bench Press
3 × 10–12 · full range of motion
Chest + Triceps
▼
  1. Lie flat on bench, dumbbell each hand at chest level, palms facing feet.
  2. Feet firm on floor. Shoulder blades together and down ("put them in your back pockets").
  3. Press up until arms nearly straight (don't lock). Dumbbells come slightly together at top.
  4. Lower slowly (3 sec) until upper arms slightly below parallel.
💡 Start 10–12 lb dumbbells. Shoulder blade squeeze protects shoulders and makes you stronger. Keep slight arch in lower back.
4
Walking Lunges
3 × 10 each · dumbbells · long stride for glutes
Glutes
▼
  1. Dumbbells at sides. Take a LARGE step forward — bigger than you think.
  2. Lower back knee toward floor until both knees ~90°.
  3. Drive through front heel to step into next lunge.
  4. Alternate legs each step. 10 per leg = 20 total steps.
💡 Longer strides = more glute. Shorter = more quad. Take BIG steps and lean torso slightly forward. Start 8–10 lbs.
Arm + Core Finisher
5
Bicep 21s
2 rounds · 7 bottom half, 7 top half, 7 full · burn city
Biceps
▼
  1. Dumbbells (or EZ-bar) at sides.
  2. First 7: Bottom to halfway (elbows at 90°), back down. × 7.
  3. Next 7: Halfway to shoulders, back to halfway. × 7.
  4. Final 7: Full curls bottom to top. × 7.
  5. That's 1 round (21 reps). Rest 60 sec, do round 2.
💡 These are brutal. Use ~60% of your normal curl weight. Biceps should be on fire by the full-range set.
6
Tricep Rope Pushdowns
2 × 15 · squeeze & spread the rope at bottom
Triceps
▼
  1. Rope on high cable. Grab each end, overhand grip.
  2. Stand close, elbows pinned to sides, forearms parallel to floor.
  3. Push rope down by straightening arms. At bottom, SPREAD the rope apart and squeeze triceps 1 sec.
  4. Slowly return. Elbows don't move — only forearms.
💡 The "spread" at bottom hits the lateral tricep head — visible from the front. Don't lean over the rope; stand upright.
7
Ab Wheel or Stability Ball Rollout
2 × 10 · slow extension · brace the whole time
Core
▼
Ab Wheel:
  1. Kneel on mat, grip wheel handles under shoulders.
  2. Brace core hard. Slowly roll forward, extending body.
  3. Only go as far as you can without lower back sagging (might be only a foot or two).
  4. Pull wheel back by squeezing abs.
Stability Ball (easier):
  1. Kneel behind ball, forearms on top. Roll forward extending arms. Roll back with abs.
💡 Start with stability ball if new to rollouts. If lower back arches at any point, you've gone too far — shorten range.

Key Principles
What makes this program work
  • 🍑
    Glutes 2× per week — Two sessions with different movement patterns + progressive overload is the fastest path to growth.
  • 🧠
    Mind-muscle connection — Slow down on glute & arm work. A 2-sec squeeze beats heaving heavy weight.
  • 🎯
    Flat stomach = core + nutrition — Can't spot-reduce, but anti-rotation exercises (Pallof press, dead bugs) build a tight midsection. Calories handle the rest.
  • 📈
    Progressive overload — Every 1–2 weeks, add 2.5–5 lbs OR 1–2 reps. Track weights in your phone.
  • 😴
    Recovery matters — 7–8 hrs sleep. Muscle grows during rest. Sat & Sun off are part of the plan.
  • 💧
    Hydration — Body water at 57.3% is solid. Aim ~80–100 oz/day, more on training days in Houston heat.

Where you're headed
Weeks 1–4Learn form · build habit · use plan as-is

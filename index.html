Glute Focus A
Hip-hinge dominant · heavy compounds
~50 min
Warm-Up (5 min)
W
Banded Glute Bridges + Clamshells
2 × 15 each · light band · activation primer
Activation
▼
Glute Bridges:
  1. Lie on back, knees bent, feet flat hip-width. Band just above knees.
  2. Press feet into floor, drive hips up, push knees out against band.
  3. Squeeze glutes 1 sec at top, slowly lower.
Clamshells:
  1. Side-lying, knees bent 90°, feet stacked, band above knees.
  2. Open top knee toward ceiling keeping feet together.
  3. Hold 1 sec, close slowly. All reps one side, then switch.
💡 Light resistance only — focus on feeling your glutes fire before heavy work.
📊 EMG research confirms activation exercises before heavy lifts improve mind-muscle connection and increase glute recruitment in subsequent exercises.
Working Sets
1
Barbell Hip Thrust
3 × 10–12 · 2 sec pause at top · squeeze hard
Glutes
▼
  1. Upper back against bench. Roll barbell over hips (use bar pad).
  2. Feet flat, shoulder-width, knees ~90° at top.
  3. Drive through heels, squeeze glutes until torso parallel.
  4. Hold 2 sec — squeeze as hard as you can.
  5. Lower slowly (3 sec). Don't rest on floor between reps.
💡 Start with just the bar or bodyweight. Chin tucked — don't arch neck.
📊 2025 Frontiers in Physiology meta-analysis (12 studies): "When emphasizing GMax hypertrophy, single exercises such as the barbell hip thrust should be prioritized." #1 glute builder.
2
Romanian Deadlift (Dumbbell)
3 × 10–12 · slow lowering · feel hamstring stretch
Glutes + Hams
▼
  1. Stand hip-width, dumbbells in front, palms facing you.
  2. Soft knee bend — push hips straight back.
  3. Lower along shins until deep hamstring stretch (mid-shin).
  4. Back flat, chest proud, dumbbells close to legs.
  5. Drive hips forward, squeeze glutes at top.
💡 Don't round back. Shoulder blades pinched. Start 10–15 lb dumbbells.
📊 Controlled eccentric (lowering) maximizes hamstring + glute co-activation and stimulates hypertrophy through stretched-position loading.
3
Bulgarian Split Squat
3 × 10 each · lean forward 15° for glutes
Glutes
▼
  1. ~2 ft in front of bench. One foot behind, laces down.
  2. Dumbbells at sides. Lean torso forward ~15°.
  3. Lower until front thigh roughly parallel.
  4. Drive through front heel. All reps one leg, switch.
💡 Balance shaky? Start bodyweight. Knee tracks over toes, don't cave in.
📊 PMC: single-limb squats elicit 82% MVIC in glute max + 82% in glute med — one of the highest dual-activation exercises measured.
4
Cable Kickbacks
3 × 12 each · squeeze 1 sec at top
Glutes
▼
  1. Ankle strap on low cable. Face machine, hold frame.
  2. Core tight, kick strapped leg straight back.
  3. Squeeze glute 1 sec. Don't arch lower back.
  4. Slowly return. All reps, switch legs.
💡 Very light — isolation move. Lower back feeling it? Too heavy or arching.
📊 ACE: quadruped hip extensions (same pattern) generated significantly more gluteus medius activation than squats.
5
Abductor Machine (Leaning Forward)
3 × 15 · lean 30° for upper glute
Glute Med
▼
  1. Sit in machine, lean torso forward ~30°.
  2. Push knees apart, hold 1 sec, slowly close.
💡 Forward lean changes everything. Lower weight vs. sitting upright.
📊 Hip abduction with external rotation generates 79–113% MVIC in glute max. Forward lean shifts to gluteus medius for that "shelf" look.
6
Dead Bug
2 × 10 each side · exhale fully
Core
▼
  1. On back, arms up, knees 90°. Press low back into floor.
  2. Extend right arm + left leg out, hovering above floor.
  3. Exhale fully. Return, repeat opposite side.
💡 Low back lifts? Shorten range.
📊 2024 study: dead bugs activate rectus abdominis, both obliques, transverse abdominis, multifidus, erector spinae, AND pelvic floor — one of the most complete core exercises.

Arms & Shoulders
All 3 tricep heads + bicep peak & width
~45 min
Warm-Up (5 min)
W
Band Pull-Aparts + Arm Circles
2 × 15 each · shoulder mobility
Activation
▼
  1. Light band at shoulder height, arms straight. Pull apart until band touches chest.
  2. Arm circles: arms out, small circles × 15 forward, × 15 reverse.
💡 Never skip — prevents pressing injuries.
Working Sets
1
Dumbbell Shoulder Press
3 × 10–12 · seated · 3-sec lowering
Shoulders
▼
  1. Seated, back support. Dumbbells at shoulder height, palms forward.
  2. Press up, arms almost straight. Lower over 3 sec.
💡 Start 8–10 lbs. Core braced, back flat.
📊 EMG: overhead press activates anterior deltoids 79%, lateral deltoids 66%, triceps 61% — outperforms isolation raises.
2
Concentration Curl
3 × 12 each · elbow braced on inner thigh
Biceps
▼
  1. Sit on bench, lean forward. Brace upper arm against inner thigh.
  2. Curl toward shoulder. Squeeze 2 sec at top.
  3. Lower over 3 sec. All reps one arm, switch.
💡 Thigh brace eliminates cheating — bicep does 100% of work. 8–10 lbs.
📊 ACE EMG study: concentration curls produced the HIGHEST biceps activation of ALL exercises tested — higher than barbell, EZ-bar, and preacher curls.
3
Overhead Tricep Extension (Cable)
3 × 12 · rope · deep stretch at bottom
Triceps Long
▼
  1. Rope on low pulley. Face away, step forward.
  2. Hands behind head, elbows forward. Extend arms overhead.
  3. Let weight slowly pull hands back — feel deep tricep stretch.
💡 Stretch at bottom drives growth. Elbows forward, not flared.
📊 German EMG study: overhead extensions best activate the triceps LONG HEAD — the largest head that gives full, rounded arm look.
4
Lateral Raises
3 × 15 · light · lead with elbows
Shoulders
▼
  1. Light dumbbells at sides, slight elbow bend.
  2. Raise to shoulder height. Elbows higher than wrists.
  3. Lower 3 sec. 5–8 lbs is plenty.
💡 Shrugging shoulders? Too heavy. These should be humblingly light.
5
Hammer Curls
3 × 12 · thumbs up · arm width builder
Brachialis
▼
  1. Palms facing body (thumbs up). Curl both up. Squeeze, lower 3 sec.
💡 Targets brachialis — adds WIDTH from front view.
📊 Neutral grip shifts work to brachialis and brachioradialis. Pairing with concentration curls covers the full upper arm.
6
Triangle Push-Ups
3 × 8–12 · diamond hand position
Triceps All
▼
  1. Push-up position. Hands together, thumbs + index fingers form diamond.
  2. Lower chest toward hands, elbows close to body.
  3. Push up. Too hard? Do from knees — same hand position.
💡 Start on knees. Focus on slow 3-sec descent.
📊 ACE/Univ. of Wisconsin EMG: triangle push-ups produced HIGHEST triceps activation of ALL exercises — beating dips, kickbacks, pushdowns, and close-grip bench.
7
Plank Hold
2 × 30 sec · squeeze everything
Core
▼
  1. Forearms on floor, elbows under shoulders. Straight line head to heels.
  2. Squeeze glutes, brace core, breathe. 30 sec.
💡 Too hard? 15–20 sec. Too easy? Probably sagging — check a mirror.

Core & Conditioning
Deep core + rectus + obliques + cardio
~40 min
Core Circuit (3 rounds)
1
Pallof Press
12 each side · cable · anti-rotation
Deep Core
▼
  1. Cable chest height, D-handle. Stand sideways, handle at chest.
  2. Step away. Press straight out — resist the rotation.
  3. Hold 2 sec extended, return. That's 1 rep.
💡 Best flat-stomach exercise. Trains transverse abdominis — your natural corset.
📊 2025 NSCA: Pallof press is a top anti-rotational exercise, training the transverse abdominis and internal obliques that create the "pulled-in" look.
2
Bicycle Crunches
15 each side · slow · no neck pulling
Rectus + Obliques
▼
  1. On back, hands lightly behind ears. Shoulders off floor.
  2. Right elbow to left knee, extend right leg.
  3. Rotate through TORSO. Alternate slowly — 2 sec per rep.
💡 Biggest mistake = going fast. Slow down. Quality over speed.
📊 ACE EMG: bicycle crunches = HIGHEST activation of rectus abdominis AND obliques of 13 exercises tested. 248% more oblique activation than standard crunches.
3
Cable Woodchop
10 each side · torso rotation
Obliques
▼
  1. Cable high, D-handle. Stand sideways, wide feet.
  2. Rotate diagonally down (high to low). Arms straight.
  3. Core drives it, not arms. Hips stable.
💡 Slow and controlled. Don't yank.
4
Hanging Knee Raises
10 reps · no swing · curl pelvis
Lower Abs
▼
  1. Hang from bar, overhand grip. Curl knees to chest — curl pelvis UP.
  2. Hold 1 sec, lower to dead hang.
💡 No swinging = the point. Captain's chair if grip is an issue.
📊 ACE: hanging raises ranked #2 for rectus abdominis, #1 for lower abs specifically.
Conditioning (15 min)
5
Incline Treadmill Walk
15 min · 10–12% incline · 3.0–3.5 mph
Cardio + Glutes
▼
  1. 10–12% incline, 3.0–3.5 mph. Do NOT hold rails.
  2. Push through heels, torso upright. 15 min straight.
💡 Glute workout disguised as cardio. Need to hold on? Reduce speed.

Monster Walks + Fire Hydrants
2 × 12 each · band above knees
Activation
▼
Monster Walks: Band above knees, quarter squat. 12 steps forward, 12 back.
Fire Hydrants: All fours, lift knee to side at 90°. Squeeze, lower.

Working Sets
1
Step-Ups (Dumbbell)
3 × 10 each · knee-height box · heel drive
Glutes
▼
  1. Box at knee height. Full foot on box. Dumbbell each hand.
  2. Drive through top-leg heel ONLY. Don't push off ground.
  3. Stand fully, squeeze glute, lower with control.
💡 Higher box = more glute. Lean slightly forward. Don't cheat with bottom foot.
📊 PMC Systematic Review (2020): step-ups showed the HIGHEST glute max EMG of ALL exercises — averaging 125% MVIC (range 104–169%). Your most important exercise today.
2
Sumo Squat (Goblet)
3 × 12 · wide stance · knees out
Glutes + Inner
▼
  1. Wide feet, toes 45°. Dumbbell at chest (goblet).
  2. Push knees out, squat down. Torso upright.
  3. Drive through heels, squeeze glutes to stand.
💡 Start 15–20 lbs.
📊 2025 meta-analysis: squats at parallel or full ROM significantly enhance glute hypertrophy.
3
Single-Leg Hip Thrust
3 × 10 each · bodyweight to start
Glutes
▼
  1. Upper back on bench. Lift one foot. Drive through planted heel.
  2. Squeeze 1 sec at top. Lower slowly. All reps, switch.
💡 Start bodyweight. Weaker side first.
📊 Single-leg hip thrust ranks among highest glute activators with minimal momentum — ideal for fixing asymmetries between sides.
4
Cable Pull-Through
3 × 12 · hinge at hips
Glutes + Hams
▼
  1. Rope on low pulley. Straddle, face away. Grab between legs.
  2. Hinge hips back. Squeeze glutes to stand tall.
💡 Like an RDL with constant cable tension. Arms don't work — hips do everything.
5
Frog Pumps
2 × 20 · soles together · burnout
Glutes
▼
  1. On back, soles together, knees out (butterfly). Feet close to butt.
  2. Drive hips up fast. Squeeze, lower, repeat immediately.
💡 Finisher — burn by rep 10. No weight needed.
6
Bird Dog
2 × 10 each · zero torso movement
Core
▼
  1. All fours. Extend right arm + left leg simultaneously. Hold 2 sec.
  2. Return, repeat opposite. Water bottle on back — if it falls, too much rotation.
📊 2024: bird dog produced greatest increase in transverse abdominis thickness of all core stability exercises tested.

Active Recovery
Mobility, foam rolling & light movement
~45 min
Why Recovery Days Matter
Muscle grows during rest, not during the workout. These sessions accelerate repair, reduce soreness, and improve flexibility so you perform better Mon–Fri.
Foam Rolling (15 min)
1
Glute & Piriformis Roll
90 sec each side · sit on roller · cross ankle over knee
Recovery
▼
  1. Sit on foam roller. Cross right ankle over left knee.
  2. Lean toward right hip. Roll slowly over the glute and piriformis (the deep muscle under the glute).
  3. When you find a tender spot, pause and hold for 20–30 seconds until the pain decreases.
  4. 90 seconds total per side.
💡 After 2 heavy glute days, this is critical. Your piriformis will likely be very tight — breathe through it.
2
Quad & Hip Flexor Roll
90 sec each leg · face down on roller
Recovery
▼
  1. Face down, forearms supporting you. Roller under front of one thigh.
  2. Roll slowly from just above the knee to the hip crease.
  3. Pause on tender spots 20–30 seconds. 90 sec per leg.
💡 Tight hip flexors can inhibit your glutes from firing properly (reciprocal inhibition). Rolling them out helps your glutes work harder on training days.
3
Hamstring Roll
90 sec each · roller under thigh
Recovery
▼
  1. Sit on floor, roller under one hamstring. Other leg crossed on top for extra pressure.
  2. Roll from just above the knee to just below the sit bone.
  3. Rotate leg in and out to hit medial and lateral hamstrings. 90 sec per side.
4
Upper Back / Thoracic Roll
2 min · arms crossed · mid-back focus
Recovery
▼
  1. Roller under mid-back, knees bent, feet flat. Cross arms over chest or hug yourself.
  2. Slowly roll from mid-back to upper back. Don't roll the lower back.
  3. Pause where it feels tight. You may hear some gentle pops — that's normal.
💡 This counteracts desk posture from work. 2 minutes is enough to make a noticeable difference.
Stretching Routine (15 min) — hold each 45–60 sec
5
90/90 Hip Stretch
60 sec each side · both legs at 90°
Mobility
▼
  1. Sit on floor. Front leg bent 90° in front of you, back leg bent 90° to the side. Both knees at right angles.
  2. Sit tall and lean your torso forward over the front shin. Feel the stretch deep in the front hip and glute.
  3. Hold 60 sec. Switch sides.
💡 This is the single best hip mobility stretch for people who train glutes heavy. Hits external rotation, which gets very tight from hip thrusts and squats.
6
Couch Stretch (Hip Flexor)
60 sec each · back foot on bench or wall
Mobility
▼
  1. Kneel in front of a bench or wall. Place back foot up on the surface behind you (toes pointing up wall, or top of foot on bench).
  2. Step the front foot forward into a lunge position. Squeeze the back-side glute.
  3. You should feel a deep stretch in the front of the back-leg hip. Stay upright — don't lean forward.
  4. 60 seconds per side.
💡 If your hip flexors are tight (from sitting at a desk all day), this directly improves your ability to extend your hips fully during hip thrusts and deadlifts.
7
Figure-4 / Pigeon Stretch
60 sec each · deep glute stretch
Mobility
▼
  1. Lie on back. Cross right ankle over left knee. Pull left knee toward chest.
  2. You should feel a deep stretch in the right glute and outer hip.
  3. Gently push right knee away from you with your right elbow for a deeper stretch.
  4. Hold 60 sec. Switch sides.
8
Chest Doorway Stretch
45 sec each arm · opens shoulders
Mobility
▼
  1. Stand in a doorway. Place right forearm on the door frame at 90°.
  2. Step through with right foot. Rotate torso slightly away until you feel a stretch across your chest and front of shoulder.
  3. 45 sec per side.
💡 Counteracts the forward-shoulder posture from desk work and phone use. Improves bench press and shoulder press form.

📊 Weekly Review (15 min)
Pull out your phone and review the past week. This 15-minute habit is what separates people who get results from people who just "go to the gym."
  • Check your weight log: Did you track your weights for each exercise? If not, start this week. Open Notes app, create a note for each day.
  • Progressive overload check: Can you increase weight by 2.5–5 lbs on any exercise this week? Or add 1–2 reps? Pick at least 1 exercise to push.
  • Protein audit: Did you hit 100–115g most days? If not, identify the gap — usually lunch. Add a protein shake or Greek yogurt.
  • Sleep audit: Average 7+ hours? Sleep is when muscle grows. If you're under 7, this is your biggest gain.
  • How do you feel? Joints okay? Energy good? Motivation? If anything is off, adjust before it becomes a problem.
🧘 Light Stretching (15 min)
A short, gentle stretch session to prepare your body for Monday's glute day.
1
Cat-Cow Stretch: All fours. Arch back (cat), then drop belly (cow). 10 slow reps. Warms up the spine.
2
World's Greatest Stretch: Lunge forward, plant opposite hand, rotate torso toward front knee, reach arm to ceiling. 5 each side. Hits hip flexors, thoracic spine, hamstrings, and groin in one move.
3
Child's Pose: Kneel, sit hips back, arms extended forward on floor. Hold 60 sec. Opens hips and lower back.
4
Supine Spinal Twist: On back, pull one knee across body. Hold 45 sec each side. Decompresses lower back after a week of training.
5
Standing Quad Stretch: Hold foot behind you, pull heel to butt. 30 sec each side. Keeps quads and hip flexors mobile for tomorrow's hip thrusts.
😴 Sleep Prep Checklist
Sunday night sleep quality directly impacts Monday performance.
  • Set alarm for 7+ hours before you need to wake up
  • Screens off 30 min before bed — blue light suppresses melatonin
  • Room temp 65–68°F — optimal for sleep onset
  • Gym bag packed tonight — clothes, water bottle, headphones. Remove friction.
  • Monday's meals packed — grab-and-go = no excuses

Key Principles
What the research says
  • 🍑
    Glutes 2×/wk, different patterns — Mon = hip-hinge (thrusts, RDLs). Thu = squat-dominant (step-ups, sumo). 2025 meta-analysis: combined hip extension exercises significantly promote hypertrophy.
  • 💪
    Arms: all angles — Concentration curls (#1 EMG biceps) + hammer curls (brachialis width). Triangle push-ups (#1 EMG triceps) + overhead ext (long head) + pushdowns (lateral). Full coverage.
  • 🎯
    Core: deep + surface — Pallof press + dead bugs = transverse abdominis (corset). Bicycle crunches + ab wheel = rectus abdominis + obliques. Both layers addressed.
  • 📈
    Progressive overload — Add 2.5–5 lbs or 1–2 reps every 1–2 weeks. Track in phone.
  • 🧘
    Recovery = gains — Saturday foam rolling + stretching improves flexibility and accelerates repair. Sunday prep eliminates excuses.
  • 💧
    Hydration — Body water 57.3% is solid. 80–100 oz/day, more in Houston heat.

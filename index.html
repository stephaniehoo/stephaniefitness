Banded Glute Bridges + Clamshells
2 × 15 each · light band · activation primer
Activation
▼
Glute Bridges:
  1. Lie on back, knees bent, feet flat hip-width apart. Band just above knees.
  2. Press feet into floor and drive hips up toward ceiling, pushing knees out against the band.
  3. Squeeze glutes hard at top for 1 sec, then slowly lower back down.
Clamshells:
  1. Lie on side, knees bent 90°, feet stacked together, band above knees.
  2. Keeping feet touching, open top knee toward ceiling like a clamshell.
  3. Hold 1 sec at top, slowly close. All reps one side, then switch.
💡 Light band only — this just wakes up the glutes before heavy work so they fire first instead of your lower back. Don't add resistance here.

Triangle (Diamond) Push-Ups
3 sets · as many clean reps as you can
Triceps All
▼
  1. Push-up position. Bring hands together under your chest, thumbs + index fingers forming a triangle/diamond.

Month 3 Principles
Glutes up · arms down · the strategy
  • 🍑
    Glutes: push real load + add 4th sets — Your nervous system is trained now. Hip thrusts, step-ups and woodchops earn a 4th set at Wk 12; the rest climb in weight.
  • 🪶
    Arms: hold weight steady, chase reps + tempo — You can't spot-shrink an arm, but you can avoid adding size. Keep curls and tricep work at your current light loads, push reps to 15, then slow the negatives. The muscle stays toned; overall recomp leans the area out.
  • 📐
    The "smaller arm" trick is the shoulder + back — Lateral raises and rows are the only upper-body lifts we push. A capped delt and a wider back make the arm and waist look smaller by contrast — that's the visual you want.
  • ⚖️
    Recomp = patience — At maintenance, the scale may barely move while your shape changes. Judge by photos, the mirror, and how clothes fit — not the number.
  • 📈
    Only go up on glutes when form is solid — Hit the new weight but form breaks? Stay put and add a set. Form > ego, always.
  • 📸
    Take progress photos — Week 9 and Week 12. Same lighting, same angle. You'll be surprised.

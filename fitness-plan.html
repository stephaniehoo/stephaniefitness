Key Principles
What makes this program work
  • 🍑
    Glutes 2× per week — Two dedicated sessions hitting different movement patterns (hip hinge vs squat-dominant) with progressive overload is the fastest path to growth.
  • 🧠
    Mind-muscle connection — Especially on glute & arm work, slow down. A 2-sec squeeze at the top of a hip thrust does more than heaving heavy weight.
  • 🎯
    Flat stomach = core + nutrition — You can't spot-reduce fat, but anti-rotation/stability exercises (Pallof press, dead bugs) build a tight, toned midsection. The calorie targets handle the rest.
  • 📈
    Progressive overload — Every 1–2 weeks, add 2.5–5 lbs OR 1–2 reps. Small jumps compound fast. Track your weights in your phone notes.
  • 😴
    Recovery matters — Sleep 7–8 hrs. Muscle grows during rest, not during the workout. Your 2 rest days (Sat & Sun) are part of the plan.
  • 💧
    Hydration — Your body water is 57.3% which is solid. Keep it there — aim for ~80–100 oz/day, more on training days in Houston heat.

12-Week Progression
Where you're headed
Weeks 1–4Learn form · build habit · use this plan as-is
Weeks 5–8Add weight · add 1 set to compounds · push intensity
Weeks 9–12Advanced variations · drop sets · reassess body comp
A note on weight: Your body comp is already in a great range. As you build muscle (especially glutes), your scale weight may go up slightly — that's a good thing. Muscle is denser than fat. Trust the mirror, how your clothes fit, and your strength numbers over the scale. Re-measure body comp around week 8–10 to see the shift.
